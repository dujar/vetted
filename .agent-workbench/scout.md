## The pick

**Top pick — Candidate 1: the trust layer for Robinhood Chain's tokenized stocks ("is this stock token real, and what can it do to me?").**
Argument from the evidence columns, not excitement: (a) Demand is documented and *fresh* — measured impostor token, hidden blocklists, 6,092 opaque filter calls, all 3 weeks ago, plus the AMC-vs-Robinhood fight escalating 2 days ago keeps the topic in every feed during judging. (b) Why-not-fixed is genuinely structural: the operator is a regulated broker whose opacity is intentional, and generic scanners have no economic reason to build per-chain logic for a 10-week-old chain — this is not a feature request you're building for free. (c) It hits all four judge criteria with a deliberately small contract surface (registry + guard + pre-flight probes = highly auditable, which is exactly what "smart contract quality" rewards), rides the organizers' Robinhood Chain reservation for a top-3 prize, and demos in 5 minutes ("here's an impostor token; here's what our scan shows; here's a guarded swap that refuses it"). (d) Stylus fits naturally: the verification logic (beacon-slot parsing, staticcall probing, risk computation) is the compute-heavy Rust part organizers explicitly call a differentiator. First-100-users channel is realistic because the indie tooling community around this exact chain (xroot.dev, robinscan) already exists and amplifies. Weakness to defend in Q&A: retention beyond "check once" — answer with watchlists + filterer alerts (fold in candidate 6 as a feature).

**Runner-up — Candidate 2: Stylus mental-poker engine.** Maxes the Innovation criterion and is the purest expression of the organizers' "build with Stylus" call; the 145-pt HN interest vs. toy-only implementations gap is real. Chosen as runner-up purely on execution risk: a correct commit-reveal shuffle protocol in 2–3 weeks solo is the single hardest build on this list, and a broken crypto demo loses harder than a slightly-less-novel solid one. If the user has real Rust/crypto comfort, it can overtake the pick.

## Rejections (and why)

- **Yet another DEX / perp / vault / prediction market / mint-an-NFT** — saturated by structurally healthy incumbents (GMX, Uniswap, Polymarket); no wedge the incumbent couldn't close in a sprint; organizers effectively warn against forks.
- **Standalone Robinhood Chain block explorer / indexer** — robinscan.io already shipped (Show HN 2026-08-03, https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49159430); no named complaint it fails at.
- **On-chain options/structured products on tokenized stocks** — securities-adjacent (regulated space), and no named complainer found this session.
- **Orbit-chain liquidity/gas fragmentation aggregator** — could not source a single dated complaint this session (both searches on that thread failed); it would die at "who have you watched hit this?" Marked NO SOURCES rather than padded.
- **DeFAI / AI-agent trading** — no named complainer; any incumbent can bolt on AI, so the why-not-fixed column is empty.
